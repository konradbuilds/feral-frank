Full setup guide ↗
01

Download & install.

Run Frank on your computer with Node.js, or upload to PHP hosting with HTTPS. The guide walks you through either option.

Get Frank on GitHub ↗
02

Get your AI key.

In Settings, choose a provider and follow its key link. Create a key in your provider account. Check its free-tier limits or pricing first.

Your ChatGPT or Claude subscription is separate from API access.

03

Connect. Say hi.

Open frank.php on your host, or the local address shown by the launcher. In Settings, paste your key and click Save key. Once connected, start chatting.
